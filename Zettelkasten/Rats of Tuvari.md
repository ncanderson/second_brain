# Rat of Tuvari
Group dedicated to [[Tuvari Eilesa]], the mythical founder of [[Questan]]. 

Function as a thieves guild, and continue her work to pull artifacts from [[Zanhurilach]].

They has a presence in [[Zanhurilach]] when [[Cafatia Thefrinal]] and [[Crivnux]] worked to free [[Y'Ghatan]] from the [[Obsidian Spire]]. They (especially Thud) helped [[The Dianes]].

Later, after the fall of [[Questan]]'s field army and the siege of [[Gadliegh]], they did what they could to aid the people of that city using the sewer network to smuggle in supplies.
