# Liradel Indris
#### Description

#### Personality

#### History
Became the [[King of Death]] towards the end of the [[First Y'Ghatan War]]. After [[Mentu-em-Asim]] was dethroned, Liradel claimed the throne before anyone else could.

Prior to the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]], Liradel became trapped in the realm of [[Fraz Urb'luu]]. As a result, the dead rested uneasy, piling up in the [[Hold of Death]]. Her capture may have been orchestrated in part by [[Graiodhrra]] in his efforts to destabilize the [[Hold|Holds]].
