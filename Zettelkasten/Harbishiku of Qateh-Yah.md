# References

RAW:
The subject was a descendant of an aristocratic family. However, he was considered a cadet, meaning a descendant of younger sons, and never expected to inherit much. This lack of expectation drove him to succeed in ways often not seen in long-established aristocratic families.

Many of his family members were either killed or disappeared in a series of accidents. As a result, he unexpectedly inherited more wealth than he ever imagined. No suspicion ever fell on him regarding these events, which appeared to be tragic accidents. Low birth rates and survival rates were common among aristocrats, possibly due to plagues like the one before the Fourth Cement Silhawk War.

Using the survival instincts developed from his modest upbringing, he wisely invested the wealth and holdings he inherited, particularly in mineral extraction, focusing on copper. He was about 20 years older than Bellatsunat, indicating that this occurred some time ago.

Through these investments, he accumulated significant wealth and worked to restore his family's glory. Despite his success, he remained relatively isolated from the royal court and had little interest in political games.

During the hostilities between Smith Silhawk and Chaitanya, only the monarch of Shaitaiah was allowed to muster troops, with aristocrats permitted only small guards. However, as the Fourth Summit Silhawk War worsened, Harveshiko lobbied King Shiat Armagdar III to allow him to raise troops. Granted this request, he used his wealth to hire mercenaries and equip many workers and families under his care.

His forces played a crucial role in defending the capital of Shaitaiah, halting the advance of the Summit Silhawk Cavalry. This defense likely set the stage for peace, as the enemy's expansion was stopped by Harveshiko's dedicated forces.

After the war, Harveshiko became closely involved in the governance of Shaitaiah. Though never formally a minister, he was frequently consulted on economic matters. His contributions helped reverse the economic strain caused by King Shiat Armagdar III's building programs, expanding his personal territory and influence.

Harveshiko became acquainted with notable figures like Oropterra and Bellatsunat. Upon Oropterra's ascension to the throne, Harveshiko retained his special dispensation to maintain personal troops. King Armagdar III did not attempt to disband these forces, possibly out of fear of a coup, though Harveshiko never appeared to have such ambitions. He seemed content to live a successful life, advising the royal family and remaining in their inner circle.

When Oropterra became king, Harveshiko continued to advise him effectively. Eventually, he followed Oropterra into undeath.
