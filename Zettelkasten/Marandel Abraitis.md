# Marandel Abraitis
**Description:** Youngest child of [[Jurgis Abraitis]]. Married the captain of the guard of [[Mala]] to secure an alliance between [[Questan]] and [[Mala]] in the early days of the [[Cataclysm]].

She has some personal associate with the [[Rats of Tuvari]], specifically the sale of items excavated from [[Old Gerngarum]].

**History:** 
After the death of her brothers, she claimed the throne of [[Questan]], citing her rights as the leader of the most powerful noble house. This despite [[Questan]] being an elective monarchy. While she is able to trace her lineage back to previous monarchs of Questan, making such a claim is unprecedented in the state.

From an early age, Marandel was cloistered within opulent walls adorned with tapestries depicting tales of valor and glory. Yet beneath the lavish feasts and polished courts lay dark secrets; her father's burgeoning power attracted envious eyes from neighboring realms seeking to seize his influence. Though she wore silks that shimmered like sunlight on water, Marandel felt more at home scaling trees or wandering the misty woods beyond their estate, where magic pulsed through ancient roots as strong as noble blood.  
  
On the eve of her seventeenth birthday, whispers reached Marandel's ears—shadows lurking at court spoke of treachery against her family. Compelled to unveil these threats without alerting those who would protect or confine her for being merely a "duke's daughter," she ventured into arcane study under Lunafer Thistlebloom, a reclusive sorceress rumored to possess prophetic visions.
