# Orcustus
Primary political rival of [[Nuxis]], seeks a more active role for [[Mala]]. Often allied with [[Aschwinn]], but opposes his republican politics. Organized the partition of [[Aenuch]] by [[Duland]] and [[Iolsos]]. Engineered the succession crisis in [[Questan]], which she then solved by marrying [[Aschwinn]] to [[Marandel Abraitis]] and backing her claim to the throne of [[Questan]].
