# Y'Ghatan
**Description:** Born as [[Ur-Aptera]]. Last king of old [[Sheiteh-Yah]]. Lich king of old [[Sheiteh-Yah]], the Earth Salter, the Blood-letter.

#### Personality 

#### History
Born: 1833 BTR

After [[Belat-Sunat|Belat-Sunat's]] married to [[Tulâdang of Simit-Sil-Hak]], Ur-Aptera became the heir of Sheiteh-Yah. On the death of his father [[Shîyat Armagdar III]], Ur-Aptera became king of [[Sheiteh-Yah]] at 28. He quickly married his sister [[Belat-Sunat]], creating the [[Sheiteh-Yah-Simit-Sil-Hak dual monarchy]].

[[Belat-Sunat]] gave birth to Ur-Aptera's son [[Hâwaman]] in 1796. Ur-aptera was 37.

Died: ? BTR
