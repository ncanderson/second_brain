# Gora'in
Language spoken in [[Simit-sil-hak]]. This language became extinct in the aftermath of the [[First Y'Ghatan War]], when [[Simit-sil-hak]] (as well as the rest of the empire [[Ur-Aptera]] put together) was shattered.
