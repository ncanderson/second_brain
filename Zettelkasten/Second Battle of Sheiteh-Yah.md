# Second Battle of Sheiteh-Yah
#### Participants
See [[The Muster of Sheiteh-Yah]] for more details.
#### Lead-up

#### Description
In order to allow [[The Dianes]] cover to destroy [[Y'Ghatan's Phylactery]], many of the gods (but especially [[Hipheoth]], [[Mithrolan]] and [[Magor the Shovel]]) worked to bring together as many forces as could be mustered, knowing that the party would have [[Belat-Sunat]] and [[Y'Ghatan|Y'Ghatan's]] armies to contend with.

#### Outcome
While the battle raged above, [[The Dianes]] fought below in an old audience chamber where [[Y'Ghatan's Phylactery]] waited. When they freed it, [[Hipheoth]] took the phylactery and flew back to [[Hipheoth's Island|her island]], where she waited, showing herself in the guise of the [[Weeping Mother]].
