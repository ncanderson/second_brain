# Nabat

**Personality:** 

**History:** 
Born: 1805 BTR

Considered by many to be the rightful heir to [[Simit-sil-hak]], being the only son of [[Tulâdang of Simit-Sil-Hak]] and [[Belat-Sunat]]. However, on the death of [[Shîyat Armagdar III]] and the ascension of [[Ur-Aptera]] to the throne of [[Sheiteh-Yah]], [[Ur-Aptera]] and [[Belat-Sunat]] wed and created the [[Sheiteh-Yah-Simit-Sil-Hak dual monarchy]]. As Nabat had no claim on [[Sheiteh-Yah]], the dual monarchy muddied the waters of his ascension, though many nobles within SImit-Sil-Hak chafed at the imposition of Sheiteh-Yah rule and sought to kidnap Nabat from [[Belat-Sunat]], hoping to see the dual monarchy split with Nabat become the rightful king of Simit-Sil-Hak.
