**History:** 
Used to be a guard in [[Gadliegh]]. He claims that he once found his then-wife holding [[Aehelis Shava Xilonodel]] underwater. He had previously discussed giving up [[Aehelis Shava Xilonodel|Aehelis]] to priests of [[Tuvari Eilesa]], not realizing how closely associated they were with the [[Rats of Tuvari]]. He pledged himself to [[Veletana]], and was sent east to [[Drommenacht]] where he has remained. He presumed that Aehelis was dead.
