# Scale Spire
A 500 foot tall tower full of jumbled nonsense. Some valuable, some dangerous. Uneven floors and rickety stairs. Mold lies heavy in the air, woven through with decay. 

There is a trap door at the top, leading to the Queen's chamber. A bound fairie dragon is there, and will help but requires payment of 1 coin of pure iron. He vomits up a smooth purple stone - crush this, mix with liquid and drink it. 

If they do, a hag coven and frost giants are there around a stone circle - the giants have recently consumed lots of people.
