# Duland
A large nation, north of [[Mala]] and [[Questan]], east of [[Iolsos]]. During the war of [[Second Y'Ghatan War]], [[Duland]] gained all the former [[Aenuch]] land east of the [[Kielk River]].

Duland also sent troops, supplied and commanders to the people of [[Drommenacht]], assisting them as they attempted to throw off [[Jaliki]] rule.
