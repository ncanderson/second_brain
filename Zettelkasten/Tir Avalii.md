# Tir Avalii
An Elven city on eastern [[Oskora]]. Prior to the events of the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]], a great plague has ravaged the city. The elves, being naturally plague resistant, are largely unnafected but the human population is in rough shape. The elves throw lavish parties while the humans suffer. The human community is called [[Smokeveil]], on account of the mists that permeate.
#### Government
Ruled by elves, though humans are the majority.
