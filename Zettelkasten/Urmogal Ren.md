#### Religion
Centered around a cult dedicated to [[Ilfangrim]], they also consider [[Cafatia Thefrinal]] to be a mythical or nearly divine figure, as she was heavily involved in the creation of the half-orc army and their installation at [[Urmogal Ren]]. They have granted her the title of [[Archon of the Blood Loom]].

A frequent chant uttered in [[Urmogal Ren]] is:
> Who goes below does not return to card the wool that drapes across the spinners knees.

This was a chant created by [[Cafatia Thefrinal]] to keep the half-orcs from journeying too far down and possibly discovering [[The Brain Stem of the Elder Worm]].
