# Busar
A community in eastern [[Triof Thill]], near the [[Mahil River]]. The community is on the western edge of the [[Black Rabbit Steppe]], and arguably not a part of [[Triof Thill]] at all. This community is an agricultural culture that is entirely male and homosexual. [[Askuzai]] warriors who want to avoid straight men find sires here.

**Government:**
[[Epetar]] is the high priest of the [[Weeping Father]], and is the de-facto leader of the community.

**Religion:** 
A curious religion is practiced in Busar, involving rites of the [[Weeping Mother]]and [[Weeping Father]]. This rite is performed when there are visiting [[Askuzai]] who wish to bear children. The [[Askuzai]] women wait out of site until they hear weeping from the men. The Askuzai are preceded by their own mourners, and when the two groups meet a grief sacrifice of a young horse is made (which, according to [[Magor the Shovel]], used to be a human child). Following the sacrifice, everyone gets drunk and parties around a large fire until 'the selection', where the [[Askuzai]] will select a sire.
