# Skullwrankle
A drug, produced from [[Sulkleaf]] which grows on [[Dashnar]]. Much of the island's product is produced in [[Wrathton]].

## Effects of Skullwrankle
- Colors and shadows break apart and crystallize into weird aging monsters
- Passing through the belly of the ocean
- One step takes you 1000 miles
- The sun follows your movement
- A figure that is always facing away
