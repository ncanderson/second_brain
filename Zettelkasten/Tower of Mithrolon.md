# Tower of Mithrolon
At the end of the [[First Y'Ghatan War]], [[Mithrolan]] was severely wounded in battle. To ensure she lived, [[Hipheoth]] brought her to this tower, which put her into an extended sleep, ensuring she would not die. To ensure her safety, protection was put around the tower. One such protection was illusions meant to break the minds of simple thieves. When [[The Dianes]] made their way through the tower, they encountered these illusions. They began:

> With Mithrolan's footsteps echoing faintly from the dark, arched passageway, you step forward into the darkness. A chill engulfs you as if stepping through a thin sheet of icy water. Looking briefly to either side, you can see your companions are no longer with you. The kernel of confusion you have felt in your mind since entering the tower has grown into a roar, and while you are still yourself, you feel that something else has joined you.

[[Saka]]
Looking back, you see not an arch leading to the battle you just fought, but a ribbon of silver stretching into the horizon, cutting through the steppes of your home. You realize after a moment that you are not in the body you remember from a moment ago. As your awareness grows, you feel the pain from blows spreading across your body, wrists heavy with metal manacles. You see on the banks of the river a proud [[Askuzai]] warrior being forced into a cage on wheels, the one who fought like a plains bear to protect you. The harsh cries of your captors ring out in your ears, as the man with the whip makes another pass by your oar.

[[Mahmut]]
Looking back, you see not an arch leading to the battle you just fought, but farm-land stretching as far as the eye can see. Turning again in the direction you were travelling, you find you are on a crowded street, throngs of people pushing around you, buildings rising tall on either side of a dusty street. While the buildings are unfamiliar, you recognize the carvings and style of your home in them. With joy, you see a familiar face on the street ahead of you, and call out to them, but they give no response, seeming to ignore you, even when directly in front of you. Looking all around, you realize you recognize all these faces, but no one seems to see you. A shoulder strikes your back, and as you catch your balance again, another strikes you again on the other side. Soon you find yourself buffeted from all sides by the passers-by, and, grimacing in pain, fall to one knee, the sky crowded by bodies and buildings, but still no faces turn towards you.

[[Aehelis Shava Xilonodel]]
Looking back, you see not an arch leading to the battle you just fought, but some streets you remember very well from your youth. A tugging on your hand brings your attention back to the direction you had been travelling. A tall, red-headed man is pulling you through a crowd, and you realize you only come up to his waist. Pushing past a simple cart and entering the relative cool of an ally, you see a man and a woman approach. They flip a small bag to the red-headed man, who quickly turns and leaves the way he came. The pair, who might be siblings they look so similar, approach and hold your arms, gently but firmly.

[[Tupac]]
Looking back, you see not an arch leading to the battle you just fought, but a small path, illuminated by the soft green glow of bio-luminescent mushrooms. Turning back the direction you had been travelling, you see a familiar door, set against the natural stone wall that leads up into the blackness of the cavern above you. You realize your journey has come to an end, finally arriving safely at the heart of the small dwarven civilization in the [[Gness Ocean]]. Following their incorporation into [[New Gerngarum]], the annual census of all the dwarven territories expanded to encompass this wealthy land, and you were thrilled to land the position of trade viceroy for this year. As pleasantries are exchanged and the real business begins, you are surprised by the warmth of the representative. He is nearly singing as he recounts the wealth that was extracted in the past year, and you are surprised when he offers nearly double what you were expecting to bring home in tax levy. It's just a matter of details, and the next several days are spent arranging transit, trade duties, and making connections with the local nobility, who seem pleased with their new stations. As you prepare to return to [[Dol Faldihir]], the capital of [[New Gerngarum]], something brings you up short, and you have a nagging feeling that you forgot something.

[[Dannick the Daring]]
Looking back, you see not an arch leading to the battle you just fought, but the rolling hills around [[Gadliegh]], spotted with the lazy spires of smoke rising serenely from your neighbor's houses. Pushing open the door to your own house, you are surprised to see two guards, wearing black plate armour, bearing black arm-bands with a yellow sigil you've been seeing much more of recently. One of them stands with his back to you, the other holds your father's face near the hearth; you are surprised to see the mess - the stew that always sat over the fire has been flung across the small kitchen, its contents dripping from the heavy wooden table at room's center. Your mother and siblings stand against the far wall. As you enter, it is clear you have stepped in mid-conversation, as one of the strange soldiers speaks: "...use would we have for a woman like her, as many whelps as she's already spawned?" Your father's eyes dart towards you stand on the threshold, taking in the scene. With a wild voice, he cries out: "I told you he'd come home, if you don't any of the rest, take him! He's strong, just look at him! He..." a kick cuts off the rest of your father's words. The soldier you first heard speaking looks towards you with a appraising gaze, before silently nodding and striding towards you.
