**History:** 
Born: 1919 BTR

Shîyat Armagdar Nazrasiiman seized the kingship at the end of a blood civil war. Prior to his ascension, he was a priest who retained the right to keep his own soldiers. A charismatic leader, he proved popular with other leading figures in [[Sheiteh-Yah]]. Recognizing the risk the underestimating the extent of Armagdar's popularity, the king attempted to revoke his right to keep a body of soldiers. Whether Armagdar desired to install himself as king prior to the civil war, or simply rode the opportunity all the way to the throne room, when peace was won the [[Armagdar Dynasty]] sat on the throne of [[Sheiteh-Yah]].

Died: 1878 BTR
