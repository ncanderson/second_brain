# Wrathton
A city on the delta of the [[Frothwrath River]]. Due to the unsavory nature of the sulfurous river, the land was not built up for quite some time, meaning Wrathton itself is a younger city. 

**Government:**
Both [[Cheolan]] and [[Nigad]] have found it useful to let the city operate as a near-independent city-state, although both nations formally claim the city. By sitting outside the law of either nation, merchants and nobles of both nations are able to funnel distasteful business through the city.
