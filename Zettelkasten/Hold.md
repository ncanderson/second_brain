## The Holds Prior to the [[Cataclysm]]
On end, the power of the six unaligned holds cut free and drape over the world like a layer before seeping in. [[The Elder Holds]] expand, seeping into the elements they birthed. Below, the lower holds gape, massive and chaotic but reaching down into the [[Void]] suggested never observed, the barrier and fourth [[The Lower Holds|Lower Hold]].Void is breached, and you watch as the endless layers of abyss, the dragons and giants are all pulled down, down and disappear. Six figures ([[The Ancient Ones]]) stand before you, the beings that gave up their freedom to create the power that now courses through you.
