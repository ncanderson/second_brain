# Y'Ghatan's Phylactery
#### Description
In order to preserve his un-life and become a true lich, [[Y'Ghatan]] needed a [[phylactery]]. In order to bind Y'Ghatan to him, [[Mentu-em-Asim]] required that the phylactery represent something personal to Y'Ghatan. 

As part of the punishment of [[Magor the Shovel]] for stealing prayers from him, [[Mentu-em-Asim]] forced [[Magor the Shovel]] and [[Hipheoth]] to give up their child (how?), and this phylactery was a hideous fusion of flesh - Y'Ghatan and Belat-Sunat's son [[Hâwaman]] and [[Sashela]], the child of Magor and [[Hipheoth]].

Interestingly, this phylactery was encased in a box that has dwarven runes on it. I think that [[Mithrolan]] and [[Crivnux]] were involved somehow in this but I forget exactly how.

#### Making
Sacrifice and punishment, bound in flesh.
