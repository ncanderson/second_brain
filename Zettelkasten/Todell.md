# Todell
#### Government
A polyglot state, made up of several distinct cultural groups. Todell's success has always hinged on internal unity between the somewhat fractious groups vying for control.
#### History
Historically, the land that is now Todell lay north of the Empire of [[Sheiteh-Yah]]. Several states, [[Ishkibal]] and [[Ana-sin-Amid]] foremost among them, would come to form the northern border of that empire. Each of those states spoke a language distinct from the others, and while not the majority, there are still healthy populations of all of those groups. To the west, the influence of the languages of [[The Jut]] are spoken. That language family has been tentatively linked to the [[Sheiteh-Yah Language Group]], however the splintering of the [[Jut Language Family]] from the [[Old Sheiteh-Yah Language]] occurred long before the empire of [[Ur-Aptera]]. This veil of time means that the Jut speakers of western Todell share little affinity with the [[Sheiteh-Yah Language Group]] speakers of south-western Todell. The largest component of Todell comprises [[Kylänieme Language Group]] speakers, occupying the richest lands in the north and west of the nation, critically occupying most of the coastal regions.
