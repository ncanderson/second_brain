# Second Y'Ghatan War

Began roughly 3155 [[TR]].

The period in history that the PCs went through, a precise date for the end of this war is unclear, as the struggle against a freed [[Y'Ghatan]] bled into the events of the [[Cataclysm]].

...this scrap of history concerns the efforts of Mala, and describes events as the full scope of the conflict emerged.

[[Y'Ghatan]], counting on his initial strokes, is skirting [[Mala]] to the south. He hopes to avoid early battle with the sparsely populated [[Mala]]. [[Orcustus]] has already thrown an army away, bolstering [[Y'Ghatan|Y'Ghatan's]] ranks, but doing much to forestall war, making the fractious nobles of [[Questan]] fall into line. 

On the return of [[Belat-Sunat]], [[Y'Ghatan]] has realized that his time may be running out. The greater part of his half-orc army has been spent, re-raised as the dead. [[Gnaw]] has retreated with the remainder.

#### Major Theatres
At some point, a large army of half-orcs departs from [[Urmogal Ren]] and marches towards [[Mala]]. This army is able to move very fast, making 25-30 miles per day instead of the expected 12-15.
