# Aenuch
A nation on [[Oskora]]. During the struggles against [[Y'Ghatan]], the wizards of [[Mala]] (mostly [[Orcustus]]) were concerned that their power would be spent countering [[Y'Ghatan]] and his powerful ancient allies, leaving too little strength for the small armies of Mala to oppose. [[Orcustus]] spent years maneuvering and plotting in an attempt to shore up this mundane power (though many thought she was just trying to build her own empire). During the time of the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]], Aenuch was very poor, and it took little convincing for [[Orcustus]] to persuade [[Iolsos]] and [[Duland]] to move against Aenuch. As a result, Aenuch was partitioned at the [[Kielk River]], with [[Duland]] taking the eastern shores, [[Iolsos]] all the land to the west of the river. 

Ultimately, the comets that struck [[Oskora]] and the ensuing [[Cataclysm]] caused enough destruction that no meaningful response to this partitioning was ever accomplished.
