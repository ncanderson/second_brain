# Hipheoth
**Description:** She is not too huge on her divine powers being channelled for the purpose of raising the dead. [[Dannick the Daring|Dannick]] is aware that his morbid obsessions (that have arisen as a trauma response to their experiences in [[Old Gerngarum|The Below]]) may give her cause to test/abandon/call him and he doesn't yet know if this is a phase he'll get over or if he is beginning to suspect that the deities of the [[Hold of Death]] may have more to offer by way of power. He Believes earnestly that he would still be tapping into that greater power to the benefits of the (lawful good) honest, hard-working farming ethics he was raised with.
