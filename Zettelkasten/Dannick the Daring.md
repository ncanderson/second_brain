# Dannick the Daring
Human cleric

Dannick's maternal grandfather may likely have been one of the [[Askuzai]] sons sold into slavery who was rescued from bondate in [[Merrowmeadow]] by an order of nuns and sent south to [[Gadliegh]] where he met Dannick's maternal grandmother, who was just about to join the nuns order when they fell in love and found work at a local inn. Dannick is not sure how [[Saka]] would take this bit of family history and as they aren't 100% sure of the tale, it's just the nun's best guess.

Dannick's family farmed primarily grains and [[Gadliegh Greys]].

At one point, he gave up all the happy memories about his family as part of a deal that I forgot about. 

Mother - Leopolra
Her mother - Ramanta Vidionys. Possibly related to [[Akatyabolsha]].

His barber in [[Gadliegh]] is [[Stepa]].
