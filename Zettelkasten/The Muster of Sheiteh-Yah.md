# The Muster of Sheiteh-Yah

When [[The Dianes]] had found and dealt with all the pieces of [[Y'Ghatan|Y'Ghatan's]] soul in the form of various [[phylactery|phylactieries]] A great battle took place over the ruins of [[Sheiteh-Yah]] while the group delved below to deal with [[Y'Ghatan]]. This battle gathered a number of different groups under one banner, brought together by the gods to help the destruction of that fell lich.

Forces were drawn from across [[Oskora]].
