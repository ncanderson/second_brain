# Mala

#### Government
Wizard council. An extremely fractious form of government that is little concerned with day-to-day affairs, being more a vehicle for old wizards to hear themselves talk. Most of the real leadership of Mala occurs at the local level, but the raw power of the wizard council ensures that the occasional issue that rises to their attention is easily dealt with. 

#### History
During the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]], Mala had firm allies in [[Questan]], [[Duland]], [[Iolsos]] and [[Obraria]], mostly due to the machinations of [[Orcustus]]. However, [[The Partitioning of Aenuch]] drew the ire of [[Abia]], [[Aedsa]] and [[Ulei]], causing them to become openly hostile. Open war with [[Mala]] would likely draw [[Todell]] and [[Feland]] in the conflict as well. [[Orcustus]] has gambled that the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]] will divert attention from the partitioning long enough that it won't matter.

#### Economy
The unassailable power of the wizards has long meant that their mis-management of their populace has been met with anger but little effective resistance. When [[Aschwinn]] departed for [[Questan]], the resulting power vacuum meant that in the period leading up to the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]] were particularly tumultuous, coming close to open rebellion.
