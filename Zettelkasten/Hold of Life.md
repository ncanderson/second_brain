# Hold of Life
The realm of Life, and home of the gods. Also known as Usathra.

Somehow, [[Iolsos]] has found a way to transport people to this hold, and uses it as a prison camp to extract natural resources. Most such people originate fro [[Denkine]].
#### Government        
- Queen: [[Vorphos]]
- Mason: [[Nidathan]]
- Champion: [[Hunazios]]
- Priest: [[Kelrias the Ancient]]
- Soldier: [[Hipheoth]]
