# Injulester
A [[fey]] city. The city where they don't believe in children. Sometimes home of [[Miss Beta]]. A brutal and cruel city, on the border of the material plane, it often attracts people who want to trade in illicit things. The city is dominated by the [[Scale Spire]], a massive spire 500 feet tall.
