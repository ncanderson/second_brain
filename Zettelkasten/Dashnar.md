# Dashnar
**Description:** An island community, principally a monastery. In the [[Gness Ocean]]. 

**Government:** Overseen by several masters. [[Vashess]], the Master of Novices, the Master of the Open Hand, the Master of Shadow, the Master of the Elements. In the absence of the elders, the Master of Novices make decisions for the monastery if no consensus can be reached.

All novices are under the direction of the Master of Novices, a power that apocryphally extends to the life and death of the novice.

**History:**
Some monks practice [[The Obviate]], an oath of non-communication.

**Economy:** [[Sulkleaf]] is a rare herb that grows wild on [[The Roughs]], which sells for a vast sum (by the ascetic standards of Dashnar). This herb can be processed into [[Skullwrankle]], a valuable drug, so the raw leaf fetches a high price.
