# The Matter of Sheiteh-Yah
A cycle of poems written in old [[Sheiteh-Yah]], detailing the history of the nation and related figures. This cycle covers:
- [[Armagdar Dynasty]] 
- Series of four wars between [[Sheiteh-Yah]] and [[Simit-sil-hak]] 
- Creation of the [[Sheiteh-Yah-Simit-Sil-Hak dual monarchy]] 
- Expansion of this dual monarchy into the [[Empire of Ur-Aptera]] 
- The concordat arrived at by [[Ur-Aptera]] and [[Mentu-em-Asim]]
- The wars fought between [[Y'Ghatan]]/[[Ur-Aptera]] and forces supported by [[Hipheoth]], [[Mithrolan]] and [[Magor the Shovel]]

Traditionally, this cycle often includes the battle between the gods for the throne of the [[King of Death]], however that account and the aftermath diverges from tale of the nation of [[Sheiteh-Yah]], so the inclusion of those latter portions in this cycle may have been a later addition.

The primary translator of the cycle into modern languages was [[Belphegor]] of [[Cennan]].

# Books of the Cycle
The Matter of Sheiteh-Yah is divided into several books, each of these books is further divided into a series of cantos.
