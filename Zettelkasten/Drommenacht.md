# Drommenacht
A large nation between [[Mala]], [[Lake Cennan]] and the [[Black Rabbit Steppe]]. South of [[Duland]]. At the time of the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]], Drommenacht is under the dominion of the [[Jaliki]].

**Government:**
[[King Vali]] is the titular king of [[Drommenacht]], ruling from [[Sanlow]]. However, he is fully beholden to the [[Jaliki]], who enrich him greatly.
