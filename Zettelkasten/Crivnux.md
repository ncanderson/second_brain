# Crivnux
Dwarven king of [[New Gerngarum]]. During the events of the [[Second Y'Ghatan War]], he allied himself with [[Cafatia Thefrinal]], and sought to free [[Y'Ghatan]] from his imprisonment in the [[Obsidian Spire]]. Ultimately, he wants the throne of the [[Hold of Death]], which [[Liradel Indris]] has left vulnerable by her absence. As part of his play, he has laid dwarven runes on the box containing [[Y'Ghatan's Phylactery]], and hopes that the geas in those runes will allow him to command [[Y'Ghatan]]. He has had help from [[Mithrolan]] in this effort, and this is also the reason for the presence of dwarves both near the [[Tower of Mithrolon]] and near the [[Sheiteh-Yah Ward Stone]]s
