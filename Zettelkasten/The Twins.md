# The Twins
#### Description
Two identical twins, appearing in the guise of beautiful androgynous humans, but in reality demons from another realm. Bound by [[Hipheoth]] to protect [[Hipheoth's Island]] - whenever one of them leaves the other will age significantly, thought this will reverse when they are both back on the island. Whenever one of the twins dies, they will re-appear, wounded and seemingly near death, on the shorts of the island.  

Wear gleaming armor.
#### Personality
Super friendly. [[Hipheoth]] doesn't want them to guard her but hasn't figured how how to get them to stop.

#### History
Born in captivity, which is something that [[Saka]] was drawn to about them.
