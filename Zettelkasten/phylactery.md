# phylactery
A [[lich]] will frequently bind their soul to a phylactery. This is something of great importance to the lich. If the lich is destroyed, it will re-appear near its phylactery. 

[[Y'Ghatan]] created a phylactery of great personal sacrifice - see [[Y'Ghatan's Phylactery]].
