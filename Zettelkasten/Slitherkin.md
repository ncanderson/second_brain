A great bow, found in a core of solid ice at the north pole. A group of [[Hobgoblins]] is also there attempting to retrieve it.
#### Description
Red translucent horn, carved with a pattern of interlocking loops.
A (bow) Staff of Striking, with an additional +1D8 damage.
