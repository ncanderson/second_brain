#### History
She was the founder of [[Questan]]. Bored with rule, she began to seek other things to do. Eventually, she discovered  [[Old Gerngarum]], and began to pull a huge number of resources and dwarven relics from the ruins. This trade made [[Questan]] extremely wealthy. Management of this business required a growing cadre of operatives and administrators, forming the start of the group that would eventually become the [[Rats of Tuvari]]. 

Tuvari eventually became [[The Obelisk]] (I think).
