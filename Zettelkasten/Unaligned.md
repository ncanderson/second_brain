# Unaligned
Holds that sat outside the formal structure of Light, Dark, etc.

Pivotal in the [[Cataclysm]]; the gods of these holds became the primary deities of [[post cataclysm religions]].

The unaligned holds are:
- [[The Obelisk]]
- [[The Spear]]
- [[The Rope]]
- [[The Chain]]
- [[The Sceptre]]
- [[The Citadel]]
- two more I think...

Each of these unaligned holds are contained an ancient and powerful artifact, which the holders had access to wield. Each item is described on its page.
