# Sheiteh-Yah/Simit-Sil-Hak dual monarchy
Queen [[Belat-Sunat]] in the later years of the dual monarchy, prior to the events of the [[First Y'Ghatan War]].
![[glinmaleldur_a_middle-aged_black-haired_woman_from_ancient_sume_0fcbfed1-90d4-432a-8cb7-fc4df89293b5.png]]

**Geography**:
Following the death of [[Tulâdang of Simit-Sil-Hak]], and the ascension of [[Ur-Aptera]] to the throne of [[Sheiteh-Yah]], [[Ur-Aptera]] and [[Belat-Sunat]] wed. The states of [[Sheiteh-Yah]] and [[Simit-sil-hak]] were merged into this dual monarchy, comprising the states of Sheiteh-Yah and Simit-sil-hak.

**Organization**:
Both [[Ur-Aptera]] and [[Belat-Sunat]] retained their monarchical styles, ruling not as joint monarchs of a single state, but as married monarchs of separate states. In theory, each monarch was the sole ruler over their own territory, but as the joined state grew, those distinctions became less important.

Un later years, [[Ur-Aptera]] became the dominant political power in the state, and Belat-Sunat’s role diminished publicly. Having greater skills in command and warfare, she was often in the field leading armies, which gave Ur-Aptera more clout domestically. Perhaps due to this, later accounts heavily imply that [[Simit-sil-hak]] was no more than a vassal state of Sheiteh-Yah, but it is clear that Belat-Sunat's prowess and the might of Simit-sil-hak were instrumental in the expansion of the their empire.
